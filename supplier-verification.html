Protocol 04 // Evidence-Led Supplier Review

Supplier Verification & Evidence Review

Turn supplier claims into a traceable decision record before they become a production dependency.

Direct answer

This service tests whether the identity, operating claims, critical-node exposure, and change visibility of a prospective or active supplier are sufficiently evidenced for the decision at hand. It combines traceable public information, supplied documentation, and scoped confirmation activity.

When it applies

New supplier admission

A supplier is being considered for a critical ingredient, packaging component, process, or manufacturing role.

Claim versus evidence gap

Certificates or presentations exist, but the decision team cannot connect them to the relevant operating node.

Sub-tier opacity

A Tier-1 supplier cannot make upstream origin, capacity, or change exposure sufficiently visible.

Cross-border diligence

Distance, language, regulatory context, or fragmented records make ordinary desk review inadequate.

Method outputs

  1. An evidence ledger distinguishing verified, disclosed, inferred, and unverified claims.
  2. A node map showing the dependencies that can affect the specific product or programme.
  3. A decision brief identifying what remains open before qualification, transfer, or release.
  4. Escalation recommendations where a site visit, technical review, or independent test is proportionate.

Usable takeaway

A certificate is evidence of a defined condition, not evidence that every current product, sub-tier, change, or operating claim is covered. Record the exact decision each document supports.

Scope boundary

Evidence review is not a substitute for legal due diligence, regulatory approval, laboratory testing, or a guarantee of supplier performance. BENENOVO does not present unverified findings as fact.
